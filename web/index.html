Gloria resends the packets it hears from devices that are weak here (Chinampa, Daffodils, their vital signs and link health), marked with its short name - Annabelle shows them "via ...". Packets heard stronger than the limit are left alone: that device reaches Annabelle on its own. When Gloria would otherwise sleep (night, low battery), relay on keeps it awake while the battery is above the voltage set here - LEDs and display stay off then. Below it Gloria sleeps as usual and relays only in its short awake windows.
